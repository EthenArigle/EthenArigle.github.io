This notebook is a place for ideas that benefit from being written down before they become papers: small experiments, reading notes, technical explanations, and reflections on research practice.

## Why write in public?

Research moves through conversation. A concise note can make an unfinished idea legible, invite useful feedback, and help someone else avoid repeating the same false start.

Future posts might include:

- an accessible explanation of a recent paper;
- implementation notes from an experiment;
- reflections on a conference or research visit;
- a tutorial for a method, dataset, or software tool.

## Adding the next post

Create a Markdown file in `_posts` named with the pattern `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`. Add a title, date, description, and tags in the front matter, then write the article below it. GitHub Pages will rebuild the site after the change is pushed.
